## 💡 Pro-Tips for a Flawless Video Recording

1. **Audio Quality:** Use a decent headset or USB mic. Speak with energy, confidence, and moderate pacing (around 130–140 words per minute).
2. **Clean Desktop:** Close all extra tabs, hide your taskbar auto-hide if possible, and turn off system notifications (Windows Focus Assist: *Alarms only*).
3. **Cursor Movement:** Move your mouse smoothly and deliberately. Hover directly over the buttons and numbers you are talking about.
4. **Practice One Run-Through:** Do a quick 1-minute dry run with your screen recorder (OBS Studio, Xbox Game Bar `Win + G`, or Loom) to check microphone levels before the final recording.
