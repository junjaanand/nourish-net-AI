📐 Mathematical Formulation

Standard shelf-lives assume ideal laboratory refrigeration (4°C). In real-world food redistribution, temperature swings accelerate enzymatic and bacterial proliferation exponentially:

t_safe = t_0 × exp(-k_decay × (T_ambient - T_base) / 10) × α_humidity × β_packaging
Where:
• t_0: Baseline safe hours at optimal baseline temperature T_base
• k_decay: Thermal sensitivity coefficient (e.g., 0.25 for leafy greens, 0.18 for cooked meals)
• α_humidity = 1.0 - 0.003 × max(0, H - 40) (Accelerated decay under moisture)
• β_packaging: Sealed (1.0x), Open (0.7x), Vacuum (1.5x), Frozen (3.0x)
